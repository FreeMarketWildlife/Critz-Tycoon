What’s being compared

Both options are fresh native drawings of the same concept. The larger version has additional pixels; it is not a stretched copy. Frame size includes transparent space. The measurements above report the visible front-idle artwork.

Both use the same 16-pixel tile steps, 16-tick walking pace and alternating stride holds. Zoom is equal on both sides. On narrow phones, the room views scroll horizontally together to retain native pixels; the character close-ups always stay side by side.
