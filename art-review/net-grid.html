Net Meadow

Normal steps. One button. Watch the bugs.

  • Walk cell to cell using the arrows or D-pad. Tap another direction to turn.
  • Tap A to swing with a longer windup.
  • Hold A to ready the net; release for a quick swing. Movement stays at normal speed.
  • Face the adjacent critter. Missed swings and close disturbances can send bugs flying or scuttling into tall grass.
