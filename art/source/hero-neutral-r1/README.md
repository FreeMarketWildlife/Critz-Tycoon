# NB1 initial South neutral body proposal

SOUTH IDLE FIRST ONLY. UNAPPROVED authored reconstruction, no anatomy or creator gate passed. [Review and limitations](../../../docs/reviews/M1-NB1/README.md).

`build.py` draws independent torso/pelvis/arms/legs/feet, retains the existing bald head, assigns selected exposed skin/face/hand pixels to body rendering, and partitions actual garment/hair/pack/strap pixels into separate RGBA layers. The selected Hero is read-only. `construction.json` records real part masks, ownership, contact registration, foot/leg overlap and visibility cells. `south-idle.sprite.json` is an editable diagnostic mannequin, not gameplay art.

Only the visible South contact row and retained authored head/hand sites are inherited evidence. Hidden body contours are reconstruction; the garment/shoe split is a selected-Hero-specific ownership proposal. Exact recomposition does not prove body proportions, future swaps or directional compatibility. More views/walk packaging wait for independent first-body review.
