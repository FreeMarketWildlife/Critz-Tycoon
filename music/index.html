FROM THE COMPOSER’S NOTEBOOK

A world you can hear.

Short melodies with something to say. A second voice that answers. Bending synths, glass bells, warm keys, and little rhythmic surprises. Each piece has its own story, with recurring sounds that bring it back to Critz.

The MIDI files contain the editable notes, separate named parts, tempo, dynamics, pitch bends, and suggested loop markers. The audio captures the custom synth sounds heard here; another MIDI player will use its own instruments.

This is an original soundtrack review collection. Musical daydreams about future places and friendly battles are proposals, not new playable content. These tracks have not been added to the game.
