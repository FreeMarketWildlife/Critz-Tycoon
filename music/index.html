FROM THE COMPOSER’S NOTEBOOK

A world you can hear.

A piano alone in the rain. A conversation between two instruments. A crooked little dance in five. A quiet constellation of glass notes. Every score was rewritten from the first note, with its own phrasing, rhythm, form, and arrangement.

The MIDI files contain editable notes, named instrument parts, tempo, dynamics, and section markers. There are no pitch glides. These previews render the MIDI with original sine, triangle, square, pulse, saw, and acoustic-inspired patches. MIDI does not store waveforms; another player uses its own sound bank.

This is an original soundtrack review collection. Musical daydreams about future places and friendly battles are proposals, not new playable content. These tracks have not been added to the game.
