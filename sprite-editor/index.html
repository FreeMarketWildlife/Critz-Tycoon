REFERENCE WORKSHOP

Center-column workshop

Choose the column immediately left or right of center. Add duplicates that column toward the center; remove deletes it. The highlighted column shows exactly what will change.

Large images start with their displayed pixel grid. One edited column is one canvas pixel. Use source size only to work on original image pixels. Existing left/right differences are preserved. Padding alone would not center the anatomy.

Your canvas comes first.

Drag the thin dividers beside Tools, Panels, or above Animation to resize them. Double-click a divider to reset it. Scroll over any point to zoom around it. The +/− buttons zoom around the center of your current view; Fit recenters the whole canvas. Hold Space and drag to pan. Use the top buttons or section headers to collapse anything; Focus temporarily hides all docks. Reset layout restores defaults. Panel sizes and visibility are remembered in this browser.

B pencil · E eraser · F fill · I pick · L line · R box · D shade · U lighten · H / hold Space pan · + / − zoom · ⌘/Ctrl Z undo · ⌘/Ctrl Shift Z redo · ⌘/Ctrl S save. Click Focus for distraction-free drawing. Dividers also work with arrow keys.
