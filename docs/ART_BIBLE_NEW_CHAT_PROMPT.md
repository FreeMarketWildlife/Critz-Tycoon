# Reusable prompt — exact Emerald proportions at 2× resolution

Copy the following into an asset task. Specify the requested character/sample and whether animation is in scope; this prompt itself does not authorize a full roster, world redraw or deployment.

---

For this Critz: Tycoon task, use **Pokémon Emerald's exact measured overworld proportions and camera framing at 2× linear resolution**. Treat this as a construction specification, not a general inspiration or a generic chibi style.

Use a **480×320** world raster, **32×32** map cells, **32×64** standard human frames and **32×32** frames for the applicable small NPC/item family. Battle/intro assets, if separately requested, use a **128×128** budget. Twice the width and height means four times the pixel count, not characters twice as large on screen. Preserve the same apparent scale and visible world coverage. A frame is transparent storage, not the character's painted height.

Before drawing, inspect actual pinned Emerald frames and annotate the head, face/eyes/chin, hair, hat, shoulders, torso, arms, hands, legs, shoes, stance and ground anchor. Identify the reference body family and pose used. Set every structural target to **2 × its measured reference value**. Match relative sizes, placements, overlaps and foreshortening in front, back and both profiles. Do not substitute guessed ratios, longer limbs, taller bodies, oversized footwear or newly invented chibi proportions. Missing measurements must be investigated and labeled unresolved, never passed off as exact.

For front/back idle, make the skull, torso, shoulders, eye positions, arms, hands and legs/feet bilaterally symmetric by default. Keep hair and color/shading separate; document explicit unusual-character exceptions such as Kaid's spout and handle. Check individual anatomical masks and visible limb boundaries, not just total alpha. This default does not apply to profiles or walking/action poses.

Show the measured reference and exact doubled targets before drawing. Present every new character on a 2× native-pixel grid with a prominent vertical centerline at half the frame width and numbered horizontal guides every ten rows. One square must equal one native pixel. Continue showing boy Hero at both 1× and 2×, matched in apparent size, until the user explicitly accepts the style.

Create original Critz identities, clothing, faces and hairstyles within that measured structure. Preserve the Black child Hero and established cast/canon. Use a labeled original adaptation for Kaid's pitcher body, with consistent limb scale and gait. Keep reference-game pixels out of shipped assets.

Use the extra target pixels for finer clusters, cleaner linework, richer controlled texture and expressive details without changing the measured silhouette masses or proportions. An exact 2× nearest-neighbor reference enlargement is the comparison scaffold; final original artwork may use individual 1px target-grid details. Avoid blur, antialiasing and noisy texture. Check the result at actual play size, not only zoomed in.

Do not lock the final 2× artwork to complete 2×2 source blocks. Refine contours, diagonals and material boundaries one native target pixel at a time while retaining measured envelopes, landmarks and mirrored idle anatomy. Use selective material-colored outlines and connected highlight/shadow clusters that describe volume. Avoid uniform thick borders, globally thinning every border, flat rectangular shading and random detail pixels. Include a clean native/enlarged view beside the required grid to judge depth. Exact doubled occupancy is a scaffold, not a production acceptance requirement.

When animation is requested, map the key poses to Emerald's poses. Double spatial travel/bob/offsets while retaining measured contacts, phase continuity and cycle duration. Preserve the reference timing baseline; smoother in-betweens must fit within it and be shown beside it for review. Extra pixel density alone does not make animation smoother. Inspect all directions and preserve intentional asymmetry.

Deliver actual native PNGs/editable sources, annotated reference-to-Critz measurement comparisons at matching apparent scale, nearest-neighbor enlargements, and synchronized animation/frame stepping when applicable. Report target versus actual measurements, deviations and unresolved points. Dimensions alone do not prove visual fidelity. Do not declare artwork approved on my behalf.

Follow the authoritative Emerald 2× contract over historical 20×26/24×32 budgets or earlier generic chibi formulas. Preserve task scope, original assets, story, saves and review gates. Complete the requested sample and its evidence; do not expand into unrequested work.
