# MUSIC.02 — Thirty Different Days

**Thirty replacement compositions complete; awaiting listening review.** MUSIC.01 was rejected for sameness and excessive pitch bending. This edition starts again with new absolute-pitch scores, individually specified forms and arrangements, and fixed-pitch instruments.

[Listen to all 30](https://critz-tycoon.freemarketwildlife.chatgpt.site/music/) · [Download all MIDI files](../../../music/critz-tycoon-30-midi.zip) · [Research and theory decisions](RESEARCH.md) · [Reproducible source](../../../music/source-v2/README.md)

40:01 of original music. Thirty different quarter-note tempos (52–176 BPM), eight meters, 24 named tonal descriptions across major/minor/modal writing, and 23 procedural pitched patches. Two to four sounding instrument parts per piece; 18 drumless pieces. No pitch glides, detuned oscillators, vibrato, chorus or tape wobble. MIDI is the performance source; previews use the included custom synth. MIDI alone does not embed oscillator waveforms.
