## Full extension tileset

Original 32px hard-pixel tiles, binary alpha. Existing atlases and selected actors remain byte-identical. `assets/review/water-terrain/water.tsj` is the new Tiled-compatible extension, with stable named IDs and a gallery. Five ground families: grass, dirt/path, garden soil, paving and cliff. Eight water surfaces: four types × freshwater/ocean. Every water-to-ground and ordered water-to-other-water pair has all 47 canonical eight-neighbor masks: isolated cells, straights, channels, outer corners, inner corners and full centers. **96 pairs ×47 =4,512 transition tiles**, plus **1,880 transparent land-bank overlays** and **256 animated surface frames**, total **6,648 tiles**. Ground-to-ground transitions remain in the existing world/editor banks; no preceding tile is replaced. Deep water receives a seven-pixel recessed bank face, shallows a low two-pixel rim. Water-to-water joins are surface transitions; shallow entrances remain walkable. All four surface motions close at 32 frames ×100ms =3.2 seconds.

This tileset and demo remain explicitly labeled review art, not silently accepted adventure terrain. Existing Map Studio semantic brushes and authored maps retain their previous behavior. Broader M1/M2 gates stay with the user.
